Garage Door Spring Repair

Broken garage door spring? Same-day spring replacement across the Twin Cities metro.

  • Springs are under extreme tension. Never attempt to replace one yourself.
  • Signs of a broken spring: a loud bang, a door that won't lift, or a visible gap in the coil.
  • Torsion springs usually last about 10,000 cycles; we replace them in matched pairs when appropriate.
  • We inspect cables, drums and rollers at the same visit so the repair lasts.
